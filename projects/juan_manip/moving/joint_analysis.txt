JOINT LIMIT ANALYSIS
==================

Joint Limits (from ned2_robot.xml):
shoulder: -2.99987 to 2.99987 rad
arm:      -1.8326 to 0.610167 rad
elbow:    -1.34006 to 1.5701 rad
forearm:  -2.09003 to 2.09003 rad
wrist:    -1.92004 to 1.92283 rad
hand:     -2.53003 to 2.53003 rad

ROOT CAUSE:
The IK solver is generating joint angles that are technically within limits,
but the robot cannot achieve them due to:
1. Arm joint is trying to go very negative (near -1.8 rad, close to limit)
2. These extreme negative arm angles may cause self-collision or singularities
3. The position actuators cannot overcome the physical constraints

SOLUTIONS:
1. Modify IK solver to avoid extreme arm joint angles (keep arm > -1.5 rad)
2. Adjust target positions to be more reachable (higher Z, less reach required)
3. Add joint angle penalties in IK to prefer mid-range positions
4. Simplify the pick-and-place path to avoid extreme configurations
